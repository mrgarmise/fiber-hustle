How to play

Each deliberate tilt moves all numbered tiles in the same direction. Identical tiles combine once per move, doubling their value and adding to your score. A new 2 or 4 appears after every successful move.

Tap Enable tilt in Safari to allow motion access. Hold the phone at a comfortable angle when the sensor calibrates. Tilt left/right or tip the top of the phone forward/backward; return near your neutral position before your next tilt. Tap Recenter to set a new neutral angle whenever you change your grip. Motion controls require a secure HTTPS page and a supported browser.

Buttons, swipes, and arrow keys work without motion permission. Your game and best score are saved on this device.
