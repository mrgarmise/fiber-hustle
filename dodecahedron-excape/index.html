Prototype rules · subject to change

Four pawns begin in a shared inner pentagon: A1/A2 (blue) and B1/B2 (orange). Blue and orange take alternating team turns; each pawn can act once or wait. Actions: move to a neighboring face, change BUILD/DEFEND attitude, or BUILD one shared polygon edge.

Claim a room before leaving it. A room is awarded only when every edge is colored. More colored edges wins; inner ties belong to both teams, middle ties belong to neither, and outer rooms require a two-edge lead. Defend blocks repainting an enemy-owned edge from either of its neighboring rooms, but never neutral coloring.

There are 12 pentagons and 20 hexagons per shell. IDs such as I-P00, M-H07 and O-P11 are stable even when you orbit. Breadcrumb dots persist by team, one per room, and an equatorial ownership ring opens the next shell. No monsters, traps, shell rotation, or final winner yet.
