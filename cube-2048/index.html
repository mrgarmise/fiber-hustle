How it works

This is 2048 inside a real 4 × 4 × 4 grid (64 positions). Every move slides all numbered cubes along one of three axes. Equal neighbors merge just once per move, then a fresh 2 or 4 spawns in an empty cell.

Rotate the view by dragging the 3D cube, independently of gameplay. Use All / Layer 1–4 to see past obscuring tiles. The three colored axis guides are drawn directly along real edges of the cube. Each arrow is marked with its signed direction: X− and X+ (green), Y− and Y+ (gold), Z− and Z+ (purple). The same sign appears on the corresponding move button. The six controls always move along the labelled X, Y and depth Z axes, regardless of camera rotation: Toward goes to the front (Z=1), Away to the back (Z=4). Layer controls show these same Z coordinates.

Use the six on-screen direction buttons, keyboard arrows for X/Y, and Q/E for Z. Your board, best score, and undo are saved locally. Camera rotations never count as moves.
