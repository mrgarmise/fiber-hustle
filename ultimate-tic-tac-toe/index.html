The rulesClassic Ultimate

There are nine 3×3 boards. Win three small boards in a row to win the match.

Your move sends your opponent: Playing the top-right square in any small board sends the opponent to the top-right small board of the larger grid. The same rule applies to all nine positions.

If the destination board is already won or tied, the next player may choose any unfinished board.

Win a small board by getting three of your marks in a row. A won or tied board is closed. No extra turns for winning a small board.

Highlight: The playable small board has a bright border. Tap an empty square to move.
