How to play

Slide: Tap one colored hexagonal block, then tap an empty neighboring space. All six neighboring directions are allowed. A group of two or more matching touching blocks disappears instantly.

Rotate: Rotate the whole hexagonal board 60° left or right. Gravity is always straight down relative to your screen, so blocks tumble toward the new bottom. When matching blocks touch, they disappear; remaining blocks keep falling until settled.

Goal: Remove every block. Each slide and each rotation counts as one move. The board spins smoothly before blocks settle. You can undo any number of steps. The first five puzzles were checked by a solver; the Procedural button creates additional seed-replayable, solver-verified puzzles that need both slide and rotate. Use Next puzzle for a fresh seed, or enter an existing seed and press Generate to replay it.
