How to play

Each player starts with two little people, a home vertex, and a fixed supply of edges (12 / 16 / 20 by board size). On your turn, build one edge, remove one edge, move one person by one friendly edge, or recruit another person (up to four).

Build only where your existing friendly network or a person at home touches the new edge. Cut your own edge to recycle it, or cut an enemy edge if one endpoint touches your own network. A removed edge returns to its original owner's inventory.

A stranded person away from home tumbles off the grid and respawns at home. The saboteur earns +1 point per enemy lost. Reach the shared gold target ★ with any person to earn +2 points; then it disappears and respawns at a different vertex. Star coordinates can be toggled on. Repeating the same board state three times triggers a double cartoon explosion and briefly locks the contested edge. First to 10 points wins. Multiple people may occupy the same vertex. Home is a protected respawn point.

Start the edge selector at a person's vertex and aim toward the destination to move. You can recruit at any time, spending one turn. The three colored planes update as you move each slider.
