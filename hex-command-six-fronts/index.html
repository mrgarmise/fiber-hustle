Rules of Six Fronts

This variant uses a true hexagon-shaped map with a separate starting territory lined up against each of its six outer sides. The center is open. Choose two opposing armies, three alternating armies, or all six sides. Each active side receives 16 secret-rank units.

Before play, each human army can swap pieces or shuffle its formation. In computer mode, the other armies deploy automatically. In pass-and-play, a privacy cover appears between each setup stage and turn.

Move a non-Bomb, non-Flag unit one neighboring hex per turn, in any of six directions. Scouts (2) may travel multiple empty hexes in a straight line. Higher rank wins combat; identical ranks destroy each other. A Miner (3) attacking a Bomb defuses it. A Spy (1) attacking a Marshal (10) defeats the Marshal. Other attackers lose to Bombs.

Capture another army's Flag to annex its entire surviving army. Every remaining piece—including bombs—changes to your color and becomes yours to command. The captured Flag is removed. An army with no legal movement is eliminated. Last army standing wins. Repeated back-and-forth moves are limited.
