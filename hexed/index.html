How to play

Slide: Tap one colored hexagonal block, then tap an empty neighboring space. All six neighboring directions are allowed. A group of two or more matching touching blocks disappears. Gravity settles blocks immediately after each slide.

Rotate: Rotate the whole hexagonal board 60° left or right. Gravity always pulls downward on your screen. Solid gray stone hexes act as fixed platforms that block slides and falling blocks. After EVERY slide or rotation, unsupported blocks fall until supported by stone, another block, or the board edge. When touching blocks match, they disappear and gravity continues.

Goal: Remove every block. Each slide and rotation counts as one move. Stone platforms hold blocks while gravity pulls downward. You can undo any number of steps. The first five puzzles were checked by a solver; the Procedural button creates additional seed-replayable, solver-verified puzzles that need both slide and rotate. Use Next puzzle for a fresh seed, or enter an existing seed and press Generate to replay it.
