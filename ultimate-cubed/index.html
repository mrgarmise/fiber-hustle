How Ultimate³ works

There are 9 regions, each containing 9 small Tic-Tac-Toe boards, each containing 9 cells. Win three cells in a row to capture a board, three boards in a row to capture a region, and three regions in a row to win the match.

Routing: Your board position determines the region where your opponent must go. Your cell position determines the board they must play in inside that region.

If the required board is closed, your opponent may choose any unfinished board in the required region. If the entire region is closed, they may choose any unfinished region and board. A captured board cannot be played in again.

Classic uses one region and the familiar rule that a cell sends your opponent to the matching board. Quick Start begins with four pre-captured boards in each region, shortening the game. Tied boards block ownership but cannot form winning lines.

You can zoom out to inspect the full game. Legal destinations are highlighted; after a move, the view automatically follows the next player’s destination.
