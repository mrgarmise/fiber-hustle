Select a W cube and Z layer, then tap an X–Y square. Lines can extend along any direction in four-dimensional space, including directions that change W, Z, X, and Y simultaneously. The highlighted win appears on the 3D view when its W cube is selected.

A winning line contains the selected board size in a row: 3, 4, or 5 identical marks. All 40 distinct line directions are counted. Number of possible winning lines: 272 (3⁴), 520 (4⁴), and 888 (5⁴).

Two marks may appear far apart when looking at a single 3D cube because the winning line can cross several W slices. When someone wins, the game highlights all winning cells; use the W and Z controls to explore the line.

The computer is a tactical opponent that blocks imminent wins and prefers developing lines, not an unbeatable 4D solver. Changing size or opponent starts a new game after confirmation.
