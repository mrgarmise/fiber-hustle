Only outer boundary edges are needed. Interiors can still be captured later. Rewards stack for newly completed shapes.

Players claim one unit-length edge per turn. Completing any square or cube gives points and an extra turn. Large squares and cubes need only their outside perimeter or twelve outer edges—not interior edges or faces. Hold and drag the board to rotate it. For the first demo, boards support sizes up to 5×5×5 points and optional computer play.
