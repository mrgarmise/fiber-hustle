How Tetra & Claim scores

The playing field is a triangular pyramid (tetrahedron) subdivided into a three-dimensional triangular lattice. There are 3, 4, or 5 vertices along each outer edge, including both endpoints.

Take turns drawing one unclaimed edge between neighboring dots. A completed triangular perimeter earns k² points, where k is its side length in lattice steps. Completing all six boundary edges of a tetrahedral volume earns 10k³ points. Larger shapes count even when their interiors have empty edges. You can claim interior shapes as well as exterior ones.

If your new edge completes one or more triangles or tetrahedra, you score all of them and get another turn. Otherwise your turn passes. The highest score when all edges are claimed wins.

The 12 directional buttons cover all six possible lattice directions and their reverses. You can also tap a line in the pyramid to select it directly.
