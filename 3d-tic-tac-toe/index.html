Take turns placing X or O at any empty coordinate. Choose a horizontal layer, then tap a cell. Win by making a straight line of 3, 4, or 5 marks, matching the cube size. Lines can run horizontally, vertically, diagonally in a layer, or diagonally through 3D space.
